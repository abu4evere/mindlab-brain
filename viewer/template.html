What happened (click to jump)

    The world. Three 2 × 2 food patches regrow 200 steps after eating, and each moves twice per life. Energy falls by 1 each step; a meal gives 30. Fatigue rises outside the shelter and falls fast inside it.

    The brain. A planner on its own map memory, raised by a fading parent (copying it every 4th step for its first 400,000 steps), then learning from its feelings alone. Nobody gives it the rule "go to food when hungry".

    What to watch. Remembered food that is really gone, the blue glow pulling toward old patches, and hunger rising while it searches. Most deaths come when it knows of no food.
