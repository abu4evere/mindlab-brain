Round-table, round 1 (Mind Lab, Brain v2 step V2-2 'Goals'; a draft, not frozen). CW2 result (final, recomputed blind): once a join at the station has been experienced, steering Bob there by a WRITTEN carry reflex doubled the chains per life (+0.63 [+0.43, +0.84], 16/16), while steering through the walker's own planner (marking the station as food in its map) gave only +0.11 [+0.06, +0.16], because the walker's food pull is weak when fed. The walker is W3n's DriveVIN: reward map r = W0*x + s*Ws*x + f*Wf*x + c*Wc*x + b over five map layers x (wall, unseen, food, shelter, staleness; 3x3 convolutions), s, f, c the hunger, tiredness and curiosity levels, then value iteration and a policy head; trained by actor-critic with the drive's change as reward (energy counts only below a set point of 150 of 200). V2-2 draft: add a sixth map layer (the goal: the place the consequence model's experienced value runs through, as in CW2, never from the untried bonus) and a fourth level g = that value / (2 meals), capped at 1, with new weights (channel 6 in W0, Ws, Wf, Wc, plus a new Wg over all six channels) all starting at 0, so the walker starts exactly as before (an exact zero check). Then adult fine-tuning from each RMS kept copy with its own actor-critic rule and its own body reward (no new reward), at most 1M steps (length from a 2-seed pilot), in CW2's world with the consequence model live. Arms (16 seeds, test on 50 fresh CW2 lands): GOAL; GOAL-NOFLOOR (the written survival floor removed for the goal pull: learned arbitration as a named sub-test); FT-NOGOAL (same fine-tuning, goal layer always empty: controls for extra training); SHAM-GOAL (fine-tuned with a distance-matched wrong place); reference: CW2's carry reflex and no-guide re-run. Primary: chains per life GOAL - FT-NOGOAL. Retirement test: GOAL non-inferior to the carry reflex (lower end above -0.2 chains) means the reflex can be retired. No-loss: the Bob Profile vs the RMS parent, plain-land survival above -0.03, an unseen evaluation world. Compute: about 64 fine-tuning runs of ~4 h on cloud VMs whose credits end ~19-20 Dec; checkpoints every 100k steps. Risk: the drive rewards energy only below 150, so a meal of C eaten when fed gives little reward.
