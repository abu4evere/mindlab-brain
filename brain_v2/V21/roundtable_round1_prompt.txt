Round-table, round 1 (Mind Lab, Brain v2 step V2-1 'Revise'; a draft, not frozen). W3r showed Bob learns within a life what each colour does (blocked, fed, rested counted per colour code; survival 0.859 vs 0.211), but after a mid-life swap of the wall and food colours survival falls to 0.250 (-0.609 [-0.642, -0.575]): the count table never forgets. CW1's consequence model is also plain counts. V2-1 adds one part, a revision rule, with no retraining (frozen RMS walker, test lives only). Arms: PLAIN (today's counts); SURPRISE (when an event contradicts a confident prediction, p from at least 3 fair trials and |outcome - p| >= 0.75, all of that colour's counts are multiplied by 0.25 before the event is added; for CW1's model the same on a (situation, verb) key whose usual next hand state had n >= 3 and share >= 0.75); WINDOW (every event first multiplies the counts it touches by 0.95, half-life ~14 events); SHAM-SURPRISE (the same decay at the same moments on a random other colour or key). Test A (primary): full-life survival under W3r's swap (from step 1,500), SURPRISE - PLAIN, 16 seeds x 50 fresh lands, paired bootstrap; no-loss gate: stable-land survival SURPRISE - PLAIN lower bound above -0.03. Test B (key secondary): CW1 with the joining operation changed at step 1,500; chains after the change. Fixed numbers set before data, never tuned. Claude's check against the code: scaling a colour's counts keeps its ratio pos/(pos+neg), so after the swap the OLD food colour (now on walls) keeps predicting fed until new fed trials arrive, and none can (Bob cannot stand on a wall; fed is only tested where he stands); only the blocked part gets new evidence. The new food colour (old walls) is reached mainly through the hunger reflex, which walks to remembered food cells by the true map memory, not by colour; there 'not blocked' and 'fed' arrive. So a decay that keeps ratios may revise only half the meaning. Options: decay only the contradicted consequence; or on surprise reset that colour toward 'unknown' (counts to 0, i.e. prediction 0 until new trials) instead of 0.25; or both. Questions: (1) Which revision rule: keep-0.25 of the whole colour, reset the whole colour to unknown, or decay only the contradicted consequence? (2) Are n >= 3, |y - p| >= 0.75 and the window 0.95 sensible as fixed numbers? (3) Is swap survival the right primary with stable survival as the no-loss gate? (4) Test B in the same study or its own? (5) What would make a positive result misleading? Please answer briefly with reasons, give your recommendation, and your confidence (a percentage).
