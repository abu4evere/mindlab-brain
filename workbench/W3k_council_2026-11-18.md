# W3k council (18 November 2026)

The draft (`W3k_draft.md`) was sent with the same prompt (`W3k_council_prompt.txt`) to GPT (chatgpt.com, Instant) and
Gemini (gemini.google.com). Their answers are summarised here, labelled; they are advice, not verified sources.

## GPT (summary of its answer)
- **CS is fair** as online checkpoint selection, not a hidden test, if the rule is frozen and the final test uses
  untouched lands. But the last 100 training lives are a noisy, biased signal, and taking the best of many windows
  creates a winner's curse.
- **CR at 0.05:** keep it; replay of fixed past states differs from a live parent in current states. Match the dose.
- **C3:** keep "the same time alone"; report that C3 has 30% more steps in total.
- **Seeds:** 12 is a screening size; prefer 24–32; report medians and leave-one-seed-out.
- **The primary reading:** CS − R2 measures checkpoint selection, not holding the peak. Rename it, and add an oracle
  (the best snapshot by test, after the fact) as an upper bound only.
- **One change:** add **CY**, a copy chosen by a pre-registered random rule, so that CS − CY shows whether the brain's
  own signal carries information, beyond "earlier copies beat the worn-down end".
- Its priors: CS beats R2 65%; CS beats CY 48%; C3 beats R2 60%; CR beats R2 25%; 0.80 reached 3%.

## Gemini (summary of its answer)
- **CS is fair** (on-policy, internal), but subject to the winner's curse. It is a meta-control mechanism, not a fix
  of forgetting. Windows of 100 lives cover fewer steps early (short lives), so the noise is uneven over time.
- **CR at 0.05:** keep it, for a clean comparison with CV. But sampling evenly across childhood replays early, novice
  states to a competent adult; that may drag it back.
- **C3:** "the same time alone" is right: it puts C3 at the same point of its adult decline as R2.
- **Seeds:** barely enough, and only if pairing holds.
- **The primary reading:** CS − R2 frames an engineering workaround as the main result; make C3 − R2 primary, or
  co-primary with a multiplicity correction.
- **One change:** remove the numerical noise at its source: force single-threaded, deterministic numerics, or pin
  paired runs to the same machine.

## What was taken (the assistant's decisions)
1. **CY added** (GPT). It costs no extra training: R2, CS and CY are the same run. One training run keeps three brains:
   its end (R2), the copy its own lives chose (CS), and a copy at a step drawn at random in advance (CY). The pairing is
   then exact, whatever the machine.
2. **Two primary readings, Bonferroni** (Gemini): C3 − R2 and CS − R2, each read with a 97.5% interval. CS − CY is the
   reading that decides how a CS win is interpreted (GPT).
3. **CS's window measured in steps, not lives** (Gemini): the mean length of the lives that ended in the last 200,000
   steps, so the noise is even over time. Checked from 200,000 steps after the fade.
4. **CR replays only late childhood** (Gemini): examples stored in the second half of the fade (1.2M to 2.4M). The
   weight stays 0.05 (both).
5. **Numerical determinism** (Gemini): threads were already 1. The likely cause is the processor's instruction set, so
   the maths libraries are pinned to one path on every machine (OpenBLAS's Haswell kernels; NumPy's AVX-512 paths
   off). **A determinism job** (R2 seed 1 twice, on whatever runners GitHub assigns) tests whether this works; every run
   logs its processor.
6. **16 seeds** (GPT: more seeds), affordable because R2, CS and CY share runs: 3 trained arms × 16 = 48 jobs + 2.
7. **The oracle** (GPT): the best snapshot by test, per seed, reported as an upper bound only, labelled exploratory.
8. Medians and leave-one-seed-out sensitivity reported beside the means (GPT).
