**The question,** the same text to all three, as the draft `W3d_draft.md` stood: a parent-copy childhood (400,000
steps, every 4th step, weight 0.5), then alone; arms P, PYFR, PA.

| | Gemini | ChatGPT (new chat) | DeepSeek |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fair, useful next rung? | yes | yes | yes |
| Biggest flaw | an abrupt cut at 400k, then 1.6M solo steps: forgetting, and a critic not calibrated | the parent uses an engineered map, so copying may teach a narrow imitation; a 0.50 result is policy transfer, not autonomous learning; compare D3 on matched seeds | the copy target is off-policy and fixed: too weak to install survival, or biasing the policy without teaching the value why |
| One change | anneal the copy weight 0.5 → 0 between 200k and 800k | fade the weight 0.5 → 0 linearly during childhood | strong early copying (every step, weight 1.0, first ~100k), then decay to 0 by 400k |

| PA worse than P | about 20% | 30% | about 60% |

## Council (the assistant)
- **All three agree:** the rung is fair and useful, and the teaching should fade, not stop abruptly.
- **Adopted:** ChatGPT's form, the simplest and inside the frozen childhood: the weight fades linearly from 0.5 at step
  0 to 0 at step 400,000, still every 4th step.
  - Gemini's (fading over 200k–800k) would lengthen the childhood.
  - DeepSeek's (every step, weight 1.0 early) changes the dose and the cost.
  - Both are recorded as alternatives for a later study.
- **ChatGPT's interpretive caution is adopted into the findings rule:** survival here shows that a parent's teaching
  can bootstrap a policy, not that feelings alone teach it.
- **On matched seeds:** W3's D3 used the same 8 training seeds, the same training-land stream and the same test lands,
  so the cited comparison is paired by seed.
- **The models' mean probability** that P or PYFR survives at least 0.50 is about 40%. The assistant's is 40%.
