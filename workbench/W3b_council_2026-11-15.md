# W3b council, 15 November 2026 (GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek; opinions, not sign-off)

Question: `W3b_council_question_2026-11-15.md` (the same text to all three; Gemini and DeepSeek got a one-sentence
context line first). Round 1 answers are pasted below from the chats (GPT: the main ACL chat; Gemini and DeepSeek: new
chats). Round 2 follows at the end.

## Where the three agree (round 1)
- **Step 0 first:** all three. GPT and DeepSeek: a greedy read-out that dies does not prove the values are wrong
  (greedy can be brittle); add planner-vs-head agreement on logged states and a look at the value near food and
  shelter. Gemini: also dump the per-channel contributions (hunger x food weight, curiosity x uncertainty weight)
  when hungry at step 100.
- **D3+F:** acceptable only as a labelled, quarantined ceiling (as W3's Script), never a candidate brain; freeze its
  trigger threshold.
- **Horizon:** keep 3,000 as the primary; log 1,000-step survival, median lifespan and more as pre-registered
  secondaries from the same test lives. Never redefine after the data.
- **Map memory:** keep it perfect in W3b; fading is a separate later study.
- **Compute matching:** FR and Y add gradient steps; cap or match them, and add a control (GPT: shuffled trajectory;
  DeepSeek: random replay / matched extra updates; Gemini: cap replay steps per life, total updates within ~15% of D3).
- **Store paths (state-action pairs), not places,** for FR (all three). Replay only while resting in the shelter.
- **Estimates that a learned arm reaches survival >= 0.5:** GPT 15%, Gemini 25% (50% if step 0 shows the value map
  already works), DeepSeek 10%.

## Where they differ
- **Y's window N:** GPT 8 moves; DeepSeek 10; Gemini up to 20 with a decay 0.9 per step back, stopping at the previous
  relief event.
- **Y's trigger:** GPT: surprise = actual drive relief minus the critic's predicted relief, threshold fixed on
  training data; Gemini: the same with the critic (or a slow moving average), threshold 0.15 of full-scale drive;
  DeepSeek: a running mean and sd of relief per need, event if need >= 0.8, relief >= mean + 2 sd and >= 0.05.
- **FR size:** GPT up to 32 transitions and 32 updates per rest; Gemini 16 per rest bout, 64 per life, buffer 256;
  DeepSeek one path (<= 10 transitions) per rest step, cap 200 per visit, buffer 512 paths.

## Round 2 (each told the other two's positions)
- **Step 0:** all three: yes, read-only, no new training. The greedy value-map walk plus Gemini's per-channel value
  dump at hungry states plus a planner-vs-head agreement log on logged states settle the fork (GPT also wants a
  one-step look-ahead walk; DeepSeek: if hunger x food is weak at hungry states the values are suspect, if strong but
  the head disagrees the head is suspect).
- **Y's window:** GPT and Gemini: a decaying window (weight 0.9 per step back), stopping at the previous relief event,
  at most 20 (GPT) or 15 (Gemini; the map's width). DeepSeek: 10 contiguous moves, no decay (one fewer number).
  Taken: Gemini's (two of three; 15 = the map's width).
- **Y's trigger:** all three accept DeepSeek's running-statistics rule (need >= 0.8, relief >= mean + 2 sd and
  >= 0.05), because W3's critic predicts a 64-step return, not one-step relief. Statistics across lives, causal
  (past only), reset at the run's start, with a warm-up; log event counts per life.
- **The one control arm:** Gemini and DeepSeek: random replay matched in count and timing; GPT: shuffled trajectories.
  Taken: random replay (two of three).
- **Final estimates** (a learned arm reaches survival >= 0.5): GPT 15%, Gemini 30% (50% if step 0 is allowed to fix
  a planner bug first), DeepSeek 8-10%.
- GPT's closing line: "Diagnose the planner first. Better learning cannot rescue an agent whose planning targets are
  fundamentally wrong."
