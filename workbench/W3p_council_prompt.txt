Mind Lab asks your critical review of a planned study (W3p) before it is frozen. Please answer as a critic: what is weak, confounded or missing, what you would change, and your probability for each prior. Context: our best brain (a learned value-iteration network, 379 parameters, with drives, two body reflexes, a parent's childhood, actor-critic plus self-imitation) fails detours: on a frozen probe (food behind a wall, memory fully known, no reflexes) it succeeds 0.27 vs a scripted BFS agent 1.00 and a greedy agent 0.00. Diagnosis: its learned value map, followed uphill, reaches the food in only 0-11% of detour cases. New design, MoveVIN: the planning loop is the body's own physics (4 moves; a move into a blocked place leaves you where you are), with a learned 3x3 'can I enter here?' filter on the senses (no wall label), learned discount, 30 rounds; the hand reads the 5 move values at its own cell; 236 parameters; same feelings part, same recipe and body. In a pilot (800k steps, 3 seeds) the passability filter never learned (0.87 at walls and open alike) and detours stayed low (0.15). Fix: learning from bumps: during training every attempted move is a supervised lesson for the filter (its 3x3 memory patch before the step; target = did it move), a logistic step every 64 attempts; nothing learned in tests. Pilot with bumps: detours 0.53/0.53/0.31 (vs 0.17 for the old brain), a memory probe 1.00 (vs 0.64), value map uphill 0.61-0.75, passability walls 0.33-0.53 vs open 0.99. Full study: MB (MoveVIN + bumps) on seeds 1-8, 4.0M steps, paired with the old brain's existing runs on the same seeds; primary = detour success MBS - HRS (one-sided 95% bootstrap over seeds, lower bound > 0) and whether MBS passes our level (lower bound >= 0.80). Priors: MBS beats HRS 85%; MBS passes 0.80 30%; lifespan not worse 55%. Questions: (1) Is the bump lesson a fair 'learned from experience' signal, or too much designer help? (2) Is it a confound that the comparison changes two things (the planner and the bump signal)? Should we add an arm, and which? (3) The walls are still leaky after 800k steps; would you change the bump learning rate, or keep the pilot's setting? (4) Anything else before freezing?
