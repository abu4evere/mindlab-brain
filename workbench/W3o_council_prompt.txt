Mind Lab (ACL's brain workbench) asks your critical view on a planned study, W3o "raw eyes". Please answer as a critic: what is wrong or weak in the design, what you would change, and your probability for each prior. Background: the best brain so far (W3n's HR) is a value-iteration network (379 learned numbers) planning on a 15x15 map memory with 5 input layers: wall, never-seen, remembered food, shelter, staleness. Its reward map is conv(W0, map) + hunger*conv(Ws, map) + tiredness*conv(Wf, map) + curiosity*conv(Wc, map); Ws/Wf/Wc start at zero and are learned, so it already learns which layer feeds it; but the sorting of the world into one layer per kind of thing is the designer's. It has a body with two reflexes that read the world's own (sorted) memory: a sleep reflex (walk to the shelter when very tired) and a hunger reflex (energy 80 or less: walk to the nearest remembered food). A scripted parent is copied in childhood (weight fading 0.5 to 0 over 2.4M of 4.0M steps). Training is advantage actor-critic plus self-imitation and replay; preliminary fresh-land full-life survival is about 0.90. The plan: keep everything identical (same seeds 1-8, paired with HR) except what the brain sees. Arm RE "mixed colours": the wall, food and shelter layers are replaced by three colour layers, wall=110, food=011, shelter=101, floor and never-seen=000; each layer is on for two kinds, so no layer means one thing, but the information is an exact linear mix (invertible). Arm RV "colours that change": the same three codes dealt at random to wall/food/shelter on each land, so colour means nothing across lands and only shape (food comes in 2x2 patches, the shelter is one cell, walls are lines and the border), place and experience tell them apart. Unchanged: never-seen and staleness layers, the reflexes (so the body still knows food: a named limit), the parent. Extra tests of each kept copy: bare (both reflexes off), hunger reflex off, and a test-only swap of the wall and food colours (manipulation check: a brain that uses colour meaning should do worse). Primary readings (97.5% Bonferroni, bootstrap over 8 seeds): RES-HRS and RVS-HRS lifespan on 50 test lands; a loss is "small" if the lower bound is above -200 steps (lives are 3,000 steps). Priors: RE costs little 65%; RV a real loss 75%; swap check passes 80%; RV fresh survival >= 0.80 15%. Questions: (1) Is RE informative, given that a linear first layer can undo an invertible linear mix exactly? Would you add a harder but still fair version (for example codes that are not linearly separable, or a bare plant colour), or is RE right as the clean first step? (2) Is RV fair to a brain whose first layer is a linear 3x3 filter, or is it a test it cannot pass by construction (and is that itself worth knowing)? (3) Should the primary reading be with the reflexes on (the brain's real life) or bare (the brain alone), given that the reflexes read sorted memory? (4) Are 8 seeds per arm enough? Compute is a home PC (8 cores, about 14 hours per run).
