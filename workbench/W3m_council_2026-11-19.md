## GPT (summary of its answer)

  helps; it does not show that the brain learned to regulate itself.
- **Q1 (hand-writing the answer?):** partly. SL brings a threshold, a way-finding policy (shortest path) and a stopping
  rule; the way-finding is more than a sleep drive. Fair as "innate behavioural control".
- **Q2 (learn from reflex steps?):** update the critic on them, but mask the actor's update for steps the reflex chose;
  otherwise the reflex becomes a teacher and SLS-off is confounded.
- **Q3 (the lines):** tiredness 90 plausible; energy 60 possibly too low for the trip home; calibrate on fixed diagnostic
  rollouts, never on the evaluation seeds. The urge's size is arbitrary relative to the rewards.
- **Q4 (cited control):** fair, if seeds, lands, copy rules and test procedure are identical and the new code is inactive.
- **Other risks:** take-over/let-go cycles near the hunger line; the exhaustion share should be per life.
- **One change:** add a rest-only arm (forced rest at the shelter, no way-finding), to separate the two parts of SL.
- **Its probabilities:** SLS − CS positive mean 75% (passing the interval about 45%); URS − CS 40% (about 15%); SL − R2
  80%; UR − R2 45%; SLS-off − CS 30%; SLS exhausted less 90%; 0.80 reached 8%.

## What was taken
