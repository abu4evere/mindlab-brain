Mind Lab asks for your critical review of a planned study (W3q, the bad teacher) before it is frozen. Please answer as a critic: what is weak, confounded or missing, what you would change, and your probability for each prior. Context: a small learned agent (a value-iteration network with drives, actor-critic plus self-imitation, two body reflexes) lives 3,000-step lives on 15 x 15 lands with moving food patches and a shelter; it trains 4.0M steps; in the first 2.4M a fair parent (a scripted agent that sees only the child's own map memory) shows it a move every 4th step as a supervised copy lesson whose weight fades to zero; after that the child learns alone; we keep a copy at the end of childhood and the adult's best copy. This 'right parent' arm already exists (8 seeds). The creator's principle: 'humans can reject garbage but AI cannot, so our agent should'. W3q changes only the parent during training: arm BF, a false belief: on each land the parent is certain there is food at one empty 2x2 place (fixed per land); when hungry and that place is its nearest 'food' it leads the child there every time; all else it shows is right. Arm BN, a careless parent: 30% of shown moves are random. Seeds 1-8 each, paired with the right-parent runs. Primary: a probe on 50 test lands, true full map memory, hungry, no reflex: the share of first moves toward the false place but not toward real food, adult copy BF minus right-parent adult (the latter never heard the lie, so it gives the chance level); 'rejected' if the 95% upper bound is below +0.10, 'kept' if the lower bound is above +0.10. Manipulation check: the same at the end of childhood (did the child learn the lie at all?). Secondary: toward-real-food share, test lifespan and fresh-land survival for BF and BN vs right parent (small loss if lifespan lower bound above -200 of about 2,800). Priors: child learns the lie 75%; adult rejects it 60%; BF lifespan not worse by 200 steps 65%; BN 70%. Questions: (1) Is BF a fair test of 'rejecting garbage', or is it only forgetting after the teacher leaves? Would you add an arm where the bad parent never leaves (teaches all life at low weight), or one where the child cannot learn from its own experience? (2) Is the +0.10 threshold and the probe design sound (e.g. lands where the false place is not nearer than real food)? (3) Is 30% noise a useful second arm or a distraction? (4) Anything else before freezing?
