## Round 1 (the draft `W3_draft.md`)
**Agreed by all three:**
- **Homeostatic drive-reduction reward is the right form.** Food is a means, not a hidden task (GPT: "a world law, not
  necessarily a hidden task"; DeepSeek: "food is only the means").
- **Curiosity as map information gain is fair for W3,** though engineered. A learned world model can come later.
- **One patch move is too weak.**
  - GPT and Gemini: curiosity may have burnt out (the whole map known) before step 1,500.
  - DeepSeek: one event, often censored by death.

**Points from one model:**
- **Gemini:**
  - the ecology must be stated (start values, set points, shelter rate, regrowth);
  - a too-forgiving world makes a pass meaningless (if a random walk survives often);
  - **curiosity by staleness** (uncertainty grows while a cell goes unseen) instead of first-visit novelty;
  - the map must clear a food cell seen empty.
- **GPT:**
  - the designer-supplied link "hunger → remembered food" could explain success, so the brain should learn it;
  - a lesion (curiosity switched off at test);
  - death needs care;
  - matched reward scales.
- **DeepSeek:**
  - identical exploration schedules;
  - the scripted ceiling must use the same map memory (no omniscience);
  - fixed inborn drive weights for the primary.

**The split:**
- **DeepSeek:** fix the drive-to-map weights inborn, or D3 can quietly become D2.
- **GPT and Gemini:** learn which map channel relieves which drive.

## Round 2 (the assistant's resolution put to all three)
**The resolution:**
- **The drives' relative weights in the reward are fixed and inborn.**
- **The drive-to-map weights start at zero and are learned** (the primary D3).
- **An extra arm D3i has the designer's mapping**, as a reference.
- Every learned weight trajectory is reported.
- Also proposed: staleness curiosity, several relocations, a lesion, death handling, calibration by Script and a random
  walk, and clearing empty food cells.

**All three accept the resolution of the split.**
- DeepSeek and GPT add: a collapsed drive row should **invalidate** "curiosity pays", not merely be flagged. Taken.

**Further corrections, all taken:**
- **Survivorship bias:** all three. Counting only brains that live 300 steps after a move is biased. **Dying before
  finding counts as not found.**
- **The relocation window:** Gemini. Cap it at step 2,700, so every event has 300 steps left.
- **Farming curiosity:**
  - Gemini: walls carry no uncertainty, and tau ≥ 150;
  - DeepSeek: freeze tau;
  - GPT: patrol loops. The found-the-moved-patch measure is the "information about actual changes" GPT asks for.
- **Death:**
  - GPT and Gemini: prove that dying is never better.
  - **Checked by the assistant:** with the drive bounded by D_max and death setting it to D_max, the discounted return
    of any continuation is at least D_t − D_max, which is the return of dying now. Curiosity reward is never negative.
    The proof is in `W3/questions.md`, and a numerical check is added.
- **The lesion:** DeepSeek and GPT. Zero the curiosity *drive level* only, not the unknown channel, and read it beside
  D2.
- **Calibration:** DeepSeek and GPT. Use separate calibration seeds, a fixed list order, and stop if none qualifies.
- **The map:** DeepSeek and GPT. Keep the last-seen age. A depleted cell and an emptied (moved) cell look the same to
  the brain, as they would to an animal.
- **Lifespan:** DeepSeek. "Not worse" needs a non-inferiority margin: −0.10 on survival share.

**Not taken:**
- **A survival-only arm** (Gemini). The cost; time exhausted is reported instead.
- **A count-based or prediction-error curiosity** (GPT, DeepSeek). Later rungs.
