# WG2: dreams that leave a question (plan, frozen before any code or data)

**Frozen** as git tag `WG2-frozen`, before any code or data.
- **The creator's standing instruction** (16 November 2026, about 00:4x): "do not ask me for next steps just go ahead".
- **Chosen by the assistant:** the missing link that WI named.
- **Exploratory,** no council.

## Why
WI found that dreams improved by themselves (+1.2 food per dream), but changed nothing awake (reach and pattern
unchanged; skill unchanged). A dream left nothing behind for the waking brain.

## The new part: a dream leaves a question
- **After each dream (as in WI),** the dream's most successful food patch is taken as a question: the imagined patch
  where the most food was eaten (ties: the first such patch; none if nothing was eaten).
- **Its 4 cells are marked in the waking map memory as long unseen:** their last-seen step is set 10,000 steps back,
  so W3's uncertainty there is about 1 and curiosity can pull the brain to look.
- **Nothing else changes:** no new reward, no new input channel.
  - The brain's own curiosity link decides whether it goes.
  - Seeing the cells (walking near) answers the question; their uncertainty resets as usual.
- In the silent room the real cells are whatever they are (usually empty floor). Going to look is the only possible
  answer.

## Arms (W3c's GYFR brain, 8 seeds, WQ's room 900001, living, 10,000 steps; GitHub's machines)
- **IQ:** WI's IM (dreams) plus the question.
- **References, cited and paired by seed (the same code and streams):** WI's L (no dreams) and WI's IM (dreams).

## Measures (WI's)
- **In the room:** activity, reach and pattern per 1,000-step window.
- **Questions answered:** the share of the 19 questions whose cells came into view within 500 steps.
- **After the room:** W3's 50 test lands, lifespan and survival.

## Readings (descriptive; 95% bootstrap intervals, paired; numpy seed 20261133)
1. **"Dreams now reach waking life":** IQ − IM on reach and pattern in the last window.
2. **"It goes to look":** the share of questions answered within 500 steps, against the same share for random cells
   of the same room under IM (each IM seed gets 19 random 2 × 2 blocks drawn by `[seed, 52]`, checked in IM's own
   path).
3. **"No harm to skill":** IQ − IM on lifespan after the room.

## Limits
- The imagination is engineered (WI's); the question-marking is engineered too: a dream's place made "unknown".
  Only the going is the brain's own.

## The assistant's prior
- IQ has more reach than IM in the last window (interval above 0): about 50%.
- Questions are answered within 500 steps more often than random blocks: about 65%.
- No harm to skill (IQ − IM lifespan interval includes 0 or is above it): about 75%.
