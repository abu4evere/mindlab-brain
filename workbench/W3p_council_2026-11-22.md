## Agreed by both
- **The bump lesson is fair learning from experience:** the agent gets its own patch, its attempted move, and whether
  it moved; no wall label, no route. But it is a designer-built channel (the target, the loss, the schedule are ours).
  Name it: "a learned local transition model inside a designer-specified planner".
- **The comparison changes two things.** Add **MoveVIN without bumps (M0)** on seeds 1–8 at 4M steps, to separate the
  planner from the bump signal. (Gemini's alternative, the old brain plus bumps, was not taken: it adds a second new
  thing.)
- **Keep the pilot's learning rate** (changing it now would tune to three pilot seeds).
- **Eight seeds make a 0.80 lower bound hard to reach;** keep the improvement test and the 0.80 verdict distinct;
  bootstrap over seeds, not episodes.
- Pre-register the mechanism measures (the value map uphill; passability, walls against open).
