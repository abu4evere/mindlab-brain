# W3m: the body's own mind (DRAFT, 19 November 2026)

**The creator (19 November 2026):** the brain has two minds, one ours and one the brain's own ("if we do not want to
sleep we eventually sleep"); give the AI parts that react by themselves. "alr lets test this bro next".

**Why (W3k's saved tests):** the best brain (W3k's CS) now dies mostly tired near food: 66% of deaths are "exhausted"
(moving costs double above tiredness 100), 66% "food known within 30 steps"; 47% both. Dying lives are exhausted for 28%
of their steps, surviving lives for 3% (the Script: 2%). Resting is left wholly to learning; nothing in the body makes
it rest. Rest only works at the shelter (tiredness −10 per step there, +1 per step elsewhere).

## Arms (W3k's run R: 4.0M steps, 2.4M fade, W3d's PYFR; its kept copies; 16 seeds; GitHub's verified machines)
| arm | the inborn part |
|---|---|
| **SL, the sleep reflex** | when tiredness reaches 90 and energy is at least 60, and the shelter is in the map memory, the body takes over: the shortest known way to the shelter, then stays until tiredness is 0. Hunger wins: below energy 60 it lets go. The brain learns from the steps as taken. It is part of the body, so it acts in the tests too. |
| **UR, the urge** | nothing forced; each step, tiredness above 90 costs the reward 0.75 × (F − 90)/110 (at the cap, W3h's thorn pain) |
| R2, CS, CY (cited) | W3k's run R, no inborn part. With both parts off, W3m's code trains W3k's run R bit for bit (checked), so W3k's tested brains are this study's control, on the same seeds and lands. |

Each arm gives its end brain, its kept copy (S) and a copy at a step drawn in advance (Y), as W3k. SL's kept copy is
also tested with the reflex switched off (SLS-off): did the brain itself learn to rest?

## Measures (W3k's)
Lifespan; survival to 3,000 and 1,000; fresh-land survival; the share of steps exhausted and hungry; death labels; for
SL, the share of test steps the reflex takes over.

## Readings (paired by seed; bootstrap over 16 seeds)
1. **Two primary readings (Bonferroni, 97.5%):** SLS − CS and URS − CS on lifespan (the deployable brains).
2. **Secondary (95%):** SL − R2, UR − R2 (the ends); SLS-off − CS (did the brain learn to rest from its reflex?);
   the share of steps exhausted, SLS against CS.
3. **Toward the bar:** the best arm's fresh-land survival against 0.80.

## Open questions for the council
1. Is the reflex too much like hand-writing the answer (the Script's own rest rule), or a fair test of an inborn part?
   Real animals cannot stay awake indefinitely; homeostatic sleep pressure is real.
2. Should the brain learn from the steps the reflex took (now: yes, as taken), or should those steps be left out?
3. Are the lines (tiredness 90, hunger 60) and the urge's size reasonable? Should they be calibrated first?
4. Is citing W3k as the control fair (same code with the parts off, bit for bit)?

## The assistant's prior

- Any arm at 0.80 on fresh lands: about 10%.
