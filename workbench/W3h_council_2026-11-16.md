## GPT (summary, its words where quoted)
**Verdict:** "promising experiment, but I would revise it before freezing."

**The open questions:**
1. Negative reward is fair "as a minimal functional model of pain's teaching role". Energy loss already hurts the
   homeostatic reward, so THP is penalised twice for one injury: report both parts separately.
2. Keep the thorn channel in all arms. Verify that the network can learn to use it (a positive control).
3. p = 1.0 is reasonable but uncalibrated. Pilot 0.25, 1.0 and 2.0, then freeze.
4. **Fear, as over-avoidance:**
   - food left uneaten because its route approaches thorns;
   - the detour ratio against the shortest safe path;
   - the distance kept from thorns.

**Flaws:**
1. **Survival bias:** add thorn contacts per opportunity (a thorn visible and reachable); report harm per life.
2. **THPw confounds copying with pain:** add THw (a careful parent, no pain).
3. **Calibration may make the world too easy:** require measurable thorn exposure.
4. **Avoidance may be trivial** (stimulus–response): test the trade-off, where crossing a thorn is worth it to reach
   food.
5. **Power:** 8 seeds is weak for three readings; 16–24 if feasible.

**Probabilities:**
- reading 1: 80%;
- reading 2: 45%;
- reading 3: 55%.

GPT: "The most interesting result would not be a brain that never touches thorns. It would be a brain that learns when
harm is worth risking, and when it isn't."

## Gemini (chat feb75edf; summary)
**The open questions:**
1. Keep pain as a pure scalar reward. A pain state would be collinear with "thorn seen" under the brain's own cell.
2. Keep the thorn channel. Without it, a failure could not tell pain from memory.
3. **On p = 1.0:**
   - the injury is already paid twice: through pain, and later through hunger;
   - keep p = 1.0 or set 0.5;
   - do not run a second p.
4. **Fear:**
   - path inflation;
   - the rate of eating food within Manhattan 2 of a thorn, against food farther away.

**Flaws:**
- **Calibration ceiling:** if a thorn-blind Script survives ≥ 0.80, harm is too mild for lifespan to differ. Calibrate
  so it survives about 0.40–0.60.
- **THPw confounds copying with pain:** add THw (a careful parent, no pain).
- **The channel starts at zero:** log its weight norm, so a null result can tell "pain did not teach" from "the input
  was never wired".
- **"Pain felt" equals thorn steps:** replace it with bout length (consecutive thorn steps per entry).

**Probabilities:**
- reading 1: 75%;
- reading 2: 25%;
- reading 3: 35%.
