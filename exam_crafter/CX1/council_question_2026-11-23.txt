Council question (ACL / Mind Lab, 23 Nov 2026), please answer critically, as an opinion, not a sign-off. We plan an "Outside Exam": put our current best small brain into Crafter (Hafner, ICLR 2022) as it is, untrained there, and measure its Crafter score against Crafter's random floor. The brain is MBb from Mind Lab: a MoveVIN planner with 236 learned numbers (a learned 3x3 reward map scaled by 3 drives hunger/tiredness/curiosity, a learned "can I enter this cell" filter, 30 rounds of value iteration that use the body's real move physics, softmax over 5 moves north/south/east/west/stay), raised in a 15x15 grid land with one food kind that disappears when eaten. The bridge is translation only: Crafter's 9x7 semantic view is written into a 64x64 map memory (the same fixed writer as Mind Lab); the brain reads a 15x15 egocentric window in its 5 channels (wall = any cell the body cannot step into: tree, stone, table, coal, iron, diamond, furnace, animals, monsters, world edge; lava is NOT a wall because the body can step on it; unseen; food = water, cow, ripe plant; shelter = own cell since Crafter allows sleeping anywhere; staleness 1-exp(-age/200)); drives: hunger = (9 - min(food, drink))/9, tired = (9 - energy)/9, curiosity = mean staleness over open cells; the hand samples the brain's own softmax; a move into a food cell becomes "face it, then do" (in Mind Lab stepping on food eats it); stay becomes "sleep"; there is no chop/place/craft move, so wood and tools come only by accident. Protocol as the paper: lives on seeds 1,2,3,... until 1M total steps; score S = exp(mean ln(1+s_i)) - 1 over 22 achievements; arms BRAIN vs RANDOM (uniform over 17 actions) with a 2000-resample bootstrap over lives; the published scoreboard (random 1.6%, Plan2Explore 2.1%, RND 2.0%, DreamerV3 14.5%, humans 50.5%) is for scale only because those agents learn during the 1M steps and MBb does not. Named limits: the bridge reads the semantic map so night darkness does not hurt it; water never runs out so in a smoke test it drank hundreds of times in a row while food ran out (we report this rather than fix it, since fixing it would write decisions for the brain). Smoke (tiny): random 1.08% (5 lives), brain 0.45% (3 lives). Questions: (1) Is this a fair and useful first measurement, or is something in the bridge either helping or hurting the brain unfairly? (2) Is "random over 17 actions" the right floor, or should we also add a floor that uses the same hand (random over the brain's 5 moves through the same bridge) so the comparison isolates the brain's choices from the bridge? (3) Anything you would change before we freeze it? Short answer please, max 250 words.
