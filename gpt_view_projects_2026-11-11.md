# GPT's view: other project ideas (pasted by the creator, 11 November 2026). Labelled as GPT, not a source

The creator asked GPT the same question and pasted its answer. GPT's ideas, in short:
- **Genesis:** one agent with senses, a body, memory and learning; no society, no language, no inherited knowledge. Can
  it develop concepts and goals from experience alone?
- **Embody:** a simulated body; learning to move and manipulate; physical consequences.
- **Mirror:** access to its own decisions, memories and uncertainty. Can it predict its own mistakes, and tell what it
  knows from what it merely believes?
- **Darwin:** populations of simple neural agents whose brain structures and learning tendencies evolve, with no reward
  for intelligence, only survival and reproduction.
- **Odyssey:** an open-ended environment, basic needs, no scripted mission. Can it form, revise and pursue long-term
  goals?

**GPT's priority:** "I wouldn't start five new large projects. I'd start with Genesis and Mirror." Its rule: do not call
an agent autonomous because it acts unexpectedly; test learning, adaptation, self-generated goals and independence from
scripts against simpler baselines. Its "biggest idea" is a research program in which every project tests one possible
ingredient of autonomous cognition. Its question: "Does an autonomous mind need other minds to develop, or can it become
fully autonomous in complete isolation?"
